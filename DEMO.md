# Due Process: a 90-second demo

**Opening line:** “Every productivity app lets you ignore your to-do list. Ours gives it a lawyer.”

1. Click **Enter demo court**. Open **Fold the laundry**.
2. Plead **I’ll do it tomorrow** and click **Hear the verdict**. The pigeon rules: “Tomorrow has declined to testify.”
3. Click **Object!**, then **It’s too big**. The actual sentence changes from folding three items in five minutes to folding one item in two minutes. “This task is three tasks in a trench coat.”
4. Click **Start 15-second demo sentence**. Turn on court sound if you want the pigeon to speak. Take a recess and resume to show the timer.
5. Enter “Folded one shirt” and click **I made progress**. It records evidence and keeps the case open.
6. Click **Actually, the whole task is done**. The court drops the charges and issues a printable certificate. A weekly task files one next occurrence automatically.
7. Return to the docket. Show **Closed** and **Awaiting**. Personal cases, dates, focus sessions and evidence persist when signed in; demo cases are isolated.

**Closing line:** “A deeply unnecessary legal system that occasionally gets your laundry done.”

The demo uses crafted verdicts and spends no AI credits. A personal hearing uses Claude Haiku 5.5 at low effort for tailored dialogue and a practical first step. The app still works when the AI service is unavailable.
